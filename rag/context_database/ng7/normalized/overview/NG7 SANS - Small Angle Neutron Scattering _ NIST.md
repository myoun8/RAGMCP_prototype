# NG7 SANS - Small Angle Neutron Scattering

The 30 meter long Small-Angle Neutron Scattering (SANS) instrument on neutron guide NG7 was the first new instrument to go into operation in the NCNR guide hall in May 1991. Jointly developed and sponsored by the NCNR, the ExxonMobil Research and Engineering Co., and the University of Minnesota, the instrument has undergone a number of improvements over the years to remain, along with its near-twin on guide NGB, the best such instruments in the U.S. 

Improvements, including a higher resolution 2D detector and focusing refractive lenses, have extended the Q-range of the instrument from $0.01\text{ nm}^{-1}$ to $7.0\text{ nm}^{-1}$ to elucidate structural features in materials ranging from roughly 1 nm to over 500 nm. A polarizing guide and RF flipper were installed in 2014. Fully polarized beam measurements are possible with the use of a $^3\text{He}$ analyzer.

## Specifications/Capabilities

| Parameter | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q-Range** | $0.01\text{ nm}^{-1}$ to $7.0\text{ nm}^{-1}$ |
| **Size Regime** | $\sim 1\text{ nm}$ to $\sim 500\text{ nm}$ |
| **Source** | Neutron Guide (NG-7), cross-section: $50\text{ mm} \times 50\text{ mm}$ |
| **Monochromator** | Mechanical velocity selector with variable speed and pitch |
| **Wavelength Range** | $4.5\text{ \AA}$ to $20.0\text{ \AA}$ |
| **Wavelength Resolution** | $11\%$ to $30\% \Delta\lambda/\lambda$ (FWHM) |
| **Source-to-Sample Distance** | $4\text{ m}$ to $16\text{ m}$ in $1.5\text{ m}$ steps via insertion of guide sections |
| **Collimation** | Circular pinhole collimation or focusing biconcave lenses |
| **Sample Size** | $5\text{ mm}$ to $25\text{ mm}$ diameter |
| **Sample-to-Detector Distance** | $1.0\text{ m}$ to $15.3\text{ m}$ continuously variable |
| **Detector** | $640\text{ mm} \times 640\text{ mm}$ $^3\text{He}$ position-sensitive proportional counter with a $5.08\text{ mm} \times 5.08\text{ mm}$ resolution |

### Optional Capabilities
