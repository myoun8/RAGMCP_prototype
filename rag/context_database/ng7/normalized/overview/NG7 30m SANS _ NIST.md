# NG7 30m SANS

Neutron spin filters (NSFs) can be applied to measurements requiring different field orientations. On the 30m SANS and CHRNS Very Small Angle Neutron Scattering (VSANS) instruments, the NSF can act as the analyzer for a polarized beam measurement with a horizontal field.

## Specifications for NSFs on 30m SANS

*   **Analyzer:** $^3\text{He}$ cell analyzer only (under low vacuum)
*   **Polarization:** $^3\text{He}$ polarization flipping capability
*   **Standard Wavelengths ($\lambda$):** $5\text{ \AA}$ and $7.5\text{ \AA}$
*   **Measurable $Q$ Range:** $0.015\text{ \AA}^{-1} - 0.12\text{ \AA}^{-1}$
*   **Sample Field ($H$):** $\le 1.5\text{ T}$
*   **Polarization Correction:** Four cross-section polarization correction
*   **$^3\text{He}$ Transmission:** $\le 54\%$ (for desired state)
*   **Flipping Ratio:** $\le 90$
*   **Sample Holder:** Manifold sample holder (up to 3 samples simultaneously)
