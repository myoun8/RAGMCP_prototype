# BT-1 Specifications

> DEPRECATION NOTICE: This page is no longer being updated and the information may be out of date.

## Specifications

**Maximum beam size:** 15 mm width x 50 mm height

**Collimation:**
* 60', 15' or 7' before monochromator
* 20' before sample [30' for Ge(733)]
* 7' before detectors

| Ge(311) | 60' | 75° | 5.78 | 1,160,000 | 2.079 | 50 |
| Ge(311) | 15' | 75° | 2.86 | 570,000 | 2.079 | 50 |

| Cu(311) | 7' | 90° | 0.54 | 230,000 | 1.540 | 100 |
| Ge(733) | 60' | 120° | 0.31 | 330,000 | 1.197 | 200 |
| Ge(733) | 15' | 120° | 0.20 | 200,000 | 1.197 | 200 |
| Ge(733) | 7' | 120° | 0.11 | 120,000 | 1.197 | 200 |

### Detectors
* 32 He-3 detectors at 5° intervals
* 13 degree scan range covering 2Theta from 0° to 167°

### Sample Environment
* Temperature from 0.3 K to 2000 K
* Vertical magnetic fields

### Sample Can Size
