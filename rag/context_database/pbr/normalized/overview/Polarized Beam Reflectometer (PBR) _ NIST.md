# Polarized Beam Reflectometer (PBR)

The Polarized Beam Reflectometer (PBR) is a cold neutron instrument used to study materials of interest in surface and interfacial science, including magnetic multilayers, polymer films, and artificial biological membranes. 

PBR is most commonly used for specular neutron reflectometry, a technique sensitive to the depth profiles of the nuclear composition and the vector magnetization in thin films and multilayers. 

### Key Capabilities
* **Magnetic Samples:** The use of a spin-polarized neutron beam allows for clear distinction between magnetic and structural contributions to the scattering.
* **Non-Magnetic Samples:** For samples deposited on a ferromagnetic reference layer, non-invasive contrast variation can be achieved by switching the spin state of the polarized beam, enhancing sensitivity to the compositional profile.
* **Sample Environments:** Cryostats, magnets, and liquid cells are available for environmental control.
* **Magnetic Fields:** Excellent beam polarization in magnetic fields up to 3 T is achievable using a dedicated cryogen-free superconducting magnet.
* **Complementary Techniques:** On-site x-ray reflectometry is available for additional sample characterization.

### Technical Specifications
* **Minimum Temperature:** Down to 3.5 K (using a side-mounted cryostat).
* **Maximum Magnetic Field:** Up to 3 T (using a compensated, cryogen-free superconducting magnet).
