# NCNR Guide Hall Instrument Layout

Floor-plan diagram of the NIST Center for Neutron Research (NCNR) guide hall, showing the neutron guides fanning out from the reactor's thermal/cold source (left, "BT" beam tubes) and the NG (Neutron Guide) hall (right) to each instrument's end position. Instrument labels are color-coded by status.

## Legend

- `*` — Center for High Resolution Neutron Scattering (CHRNS) instrument
- `†` — Not operating due to lack of resources
- `‡` — Under development

## BT beam tubes (reactor-adjacent, left side)

- **BT9 MACS*** — Multi-Axis Crystal Spectrometer (CHRNS)
- **BT8 Engineering Diffractometer**
- **BT7 3-Axis** — Triple-Axis Spectrometer
- **V5 Thermal PGAA** — Prompt Gamma Activation Analysis
- **BT1 Powder Diffractometer**
- **BT2 Thermal Imaging**
- **BT4 3-Axis†** — Triple-Axis Spectrometer (not operating)
- **BT5 USANS†** — Ultra Small Angle Neutron Scattering (not operating)
