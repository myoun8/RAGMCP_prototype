# 10m SANS - Small Angle Neutron Scattering

The 10 meter Small Angle Neutron Scattering (10m SANS) instrument is designed to measure correlations in spatial structure within solid and liquid materials. The technique measures deflections of a neutron beam as it passes through a sample of interest. The spectrum of deflected neutrons reveals important details such as:

*   **Material State:** Whether the material is amorphous, crystalline, semi-crystalline, liquid crystalline, or a combination.
*   **Compositional Distribution:** The relative location of structures (e.g., the location of water relative to a membrane pore, or a surfactant relative to a protein in a biopharmaceutical).
*   **Structural Evolution:** How structure evolves with processing parameters critical to end applications.

The instrument provides ample space for sample environments, allowing for the installation of equipment such as rheometers to measure the response of a fluid under shear while concurrently collecting SANS data on the change in liquid structure. These measurements connect structural observations to manufacturing processes including shear, high pressure, high temperature, and environmental exposure.

## Instrument Operation and Access
The 10m SANS is constructed similarly to the two 30 meter SANS instruments at the NCNR, with the primary difference being the maximum length (10 meters vs. 30 meters). 

The 10m SANS is operated by the **NIST nSoft consortium** (www.nist.gov/nsoft), which partners NIST scientists with staff from member companies. Activities on the 10m SANS are performed for the benefit of the consortium, including experiments with member companies and measurement development activities to enhance capabilities for members. Users wishing to access the instrument should contact the instrument scientists.

## Specifications/Capabilities

| Parameter | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q-Range** | 0.03 nm$^{-1}$ to 6.0 nm$^{-1}$ |
| **Size Regime** | 1 nm to 200 nm |
| **Source** | Neutron Guide (NG-B), cross-section: 60 mm x 60 mm |
| **Monochromator** | Mechanical velocity selector with variable speed and pitch |
| **Wavelength Range** | 4.0 Å to 20.0 Å |
| **Wavelength Resolution** | 9% to 30% $\Delta\lambda/\lambda$ (FWHM) |
| **Source-to-Sample Distance** | 3.0 m to 1 m via insertion of guide sections |
| **Collimation** | Circular pinhole collimation |
| **Sample Size** | 0 mm to 25 mm diameter |
| **Sample-to-Detector Distance** | 1.3 m to 5.2 m continuously variable |
| **Detector** | 640 mm x 640 mm $^3$He position-sensitive proportional counter (5.08 mm x 5.08 mm resolution) |
